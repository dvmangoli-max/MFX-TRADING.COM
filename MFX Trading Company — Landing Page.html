How the desk works

Three rules that don't change

The framework stays the same whether the market is trending, ranging, or moving on news.

Structure first

Every idea starts from what price has already done — key ranges, liquidity, and where the structure actually shifts — not indicator noise.

Risk defined

Stop-loss and position size are set before entry, not after. If the risk isn't clear, the setup doesn't go out.

No hype

No guaranteed win rates, no "100% profitable" claims. Just the setup, the risk, and an honest track record you can check.

Proof of work

Every setup is shared with the chart behind it.

No cropped screenshots, no results without the reasoning. If a signal goes out, the chart, the levels and the invalidation point go out with it.
